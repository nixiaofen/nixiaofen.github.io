02

中文实体制裁误命中判定规则库 False-Positive Rulebook for Chinese Entities

法律判断 · 规则文档 Legal judgment · rule document

7 类场景patterns

中文实体筛查的误报绝大多数不是技术问题，而是命名习惯问题：高频字号撞名、简繁与拼音变体、音译分歧、中资离岸架构的中英文名不一致。这份规则库把这些场景拆成可执行的判定标准与分级处置流程——这部分工程师写不出来。 Most false positives on Chinese entities are naming problems, not technical ones: generic trade-name collisions, simplified/traditional and pinyin variants, transliteration divergence, and offshore vehicles whose Chinese and English names do not correspond. This rulebook turns those into actionable criteria and a tiered review process.
